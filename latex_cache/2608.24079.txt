The constraint is not a kernel artifact. For a plain ID-parameterized output layer, fused cross-entropy kernels eliminate the logit memory outright~; but a item tower must recompute the catalog-wide tower forward and backpropagate through it every step, so even memory-optimal exact training pays per-step compute linear in catalog size , and the practical escapes buy scale by leaving the exact objective behind: caches score against stale embeddings~ and adaptive-softmax factorizations restructure the loss~.
