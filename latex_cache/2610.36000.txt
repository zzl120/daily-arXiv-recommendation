RELAY assigns a single query two coupled responsibilities: its attention aggregates the semantic representation and allocates explicit memory evidence to catalog candidates. Tokenization research further incorporates collaborative semantics, code diversity, sequence context, and joint optimization of the index and recommender, including differentiable semantic-ID learning~. RELAY retains a bounded transition memory at scoring time and routes its contributions with the attention already used for semantic readout. It also addresses a specific dependence introduced by aggregated transition counts: each training record contributes to the memory used to score that record. Target-statistic research studies related dependence between labels and derived features~.
