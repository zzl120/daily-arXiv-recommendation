When audiovisual, visual, or textual streams are missing, degraded, or semantically conflicting, uniform fusion and unconstrained token gates can propagate misleading content across iterative denoising steps. We propose CONCORD-Rec, a reliability-conditioned discrete diffusion recommender supervised by counterfactual catalog-partition utility. Given a fixed tokenizer and a partially masked SID state, CONCORD-Rec evaluates out-of-fold teacher coalitions over the exact feasible catalog support and aggregates item posteriors across the target code partition at each unresolved position. Rather than distilling raw token probabilities directly, the resulting counterfactual loss differences supervise an availability-masked tokenwise gate that allocates bounded modality residuals around a behavioral denoiser while retaining an exact behavioral fallback slot. We establish theoretical fixed-support ranking stability bounds under gate perturbations and evaluate position-level and code-conditional allocation across audiovisual and image-text recommendation benchmarks under controlled missingness, signal degradation, and semantic conflict.
