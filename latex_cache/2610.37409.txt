In this paper, we proposed MAGE, a multimodal recommendation framework that incorporates heterophilic complementary signals through offline manifold augmentation and online asymmetric graph propagation. Experiments on real-world datasets show that MAGE consistently improves over strong baselines, especially under sparse and long-tail settings. Additional analyses indicate that MAGE better organizes functionally related yet semantically distant items, highlighting the potential of manifold-aware representation learning for complement-aware multimodal recommendation. Co-purchase edges are imperfect evidence, and geometric safeguards do not certify the semantic meaning of individual residuals.
