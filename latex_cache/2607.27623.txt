The data points are commonly represented as dense feature vectors, often derived from the activation energy of a deep neural network layer . A linear search based on these features becomes less efficient as the number of dimensions or number of data points increases . To address this problem, numerous applications resort to approximated nearest neighbor search , a slightly less accurate but much faster solution. A variation of ANNS is used in product recommender systems and visual image browsing systems , where the goal is to retrieve many items similar to a selected item from the dataset.
