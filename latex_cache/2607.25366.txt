To bridge this semantic gap, emerging research integrates LLMs to empower CDSR with rich open-world knowledge. By leveraging their robust reasoning capabilities, LLMs can infer implicit item correlations and uncover shared cross-domain features, effectively aligning domains for non-overlapping users even without interaction links. LLM-based approaches aggregate multi-domain recommendation data into unified instruction-tuning datasets to train a single comprehensive model under a one model paradigm.
